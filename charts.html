🎼 Gig-Ready Charts & Transcriptions

Stop reading bulky, cluttered charts on stage. I distill complex, multi-layered pop and jazz arrangements down to high-utility, condensed charts that map perfectly to the studio recording.

  • Recording-Accurate: Transcribed directly from the reference recordings capturing the signature riffs, hits, and vamps.
